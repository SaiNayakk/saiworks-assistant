# Booking and Policies

## Walk-ins and the Queue

Walk-ins are welcome. When you arrive, scan the QR code at the reception to join the queue through WaitlistDrop. You get a WhatsApp message when it is almost your turn, so you can wait nearby instead of in the shop.
Saturday and Sunday evenings are the busiest; the typical wait then is 40 to 60 minutes. Weekday mornings usually have no wait.

## Appointments

You can book an appointment by WhatsApp on +91 98480 12345. Appointments are recommended for keratin, hair colour, facials and bridal services because they take longer.
You can ask for a specific stylist; our senior stylists are Ravi (men's hair) and Priya (women's hair and colour).

## Cancellations

Please cancel at least 2 hours before your appointment. If you do not show up twice without informing us, we will ask for a ₹200 advance for future appointments.
The bridal advance of ₹3,000 is non-refundable, but the date can be changed once for free if you tell us 7 days in advance.

## Hygiene

Combs, scissors and razors are sanitised after every customer, and we use a new disposable razor blade for every shave.

## Refunds and Complaints

We do not refund services once they are done, but if you are unhappy with a haircut, come back within 3 days and we will fix it for free.
