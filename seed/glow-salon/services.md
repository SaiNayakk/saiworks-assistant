## Bridal Packages

The bridal package costs ₹15,000 and includes pre-bridal facial, HD makeup, hairstyling and saree draping on the wedding day.
Bridal bookings need an advance of ₹3,000 and must be made at least 2 weeks before the wedding. Makeup at the venue is available within Hyderabad for an extra ₹1,500 travel charge.
