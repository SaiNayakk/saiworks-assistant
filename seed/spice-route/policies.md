# Reservations, Delivery and Policies

## Table Reservations

Book a table on WhatsApp at +91 90000 56789. Reservations are held for 15 minutes past the booking time. For groups of more than 10 people, we need 24 hours' notice.
Without a reservation, scan the QR at the entrance to join the waitlist on WhatsApp and we will message you when your table is ready.

## Private Parties

The private dining hall seats up to 25 guests. It is free to book with a minimum bill of ₹12,000; a ₹2,000 advance is required and is adjusted in the final bill.

## Delivery and Takeaway

We deliver through Swiggy and Zomato within 5 km. Direct takeaway orders on WhatsApp get 10% off and are ready in about 25 minutes.

## Outside Food and Cakes

Outside food is not allowed, except birthday cakes, for which there is no cake-cutting charge.

## Refunds

If a dish is not prepared correctly, tell the staff before finishing it and we will replace it. We do not refund orders placed through Swiggy or Zomato directly; please raise those in the app.
