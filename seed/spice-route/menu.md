## Dietary Information

- Veg and non-veg are cooked in separate kitchens with separate utensils.
- Jain food is available on request for veg biryani and paneer dishes (no onion, no garlic).
- Our food is medium to very spicy; ask for "less spicy" and we will adjust curries and starters. Biryani spice level cannot be changed.
- We do not have any vegan dessert.
