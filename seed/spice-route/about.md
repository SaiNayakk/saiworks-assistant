# About Spice Route Kitchen

Spice Route Kitchen is an Andhra and Hyderabadi restaurant in Madhapur, Hyderabad, with seating for 60 guests. We are known for our Hyderabadi dum biryani and Andhra meals.

## Location

Plot 44, Road No. 2, Ayyappa Society, Madhapur, Hyderabad 500081. We are on the first floor, and there is a lift. Valet parking is available on Friday, Saturday and Sunday evenings.

## Timings

- Lunch: 12:00 PM to 3:30 PM, every day
- Dinner: 7:00 PM to 11:00 PM, every day
- The kitchen takes last orders 30 minutes before closing.

## Payments

We accept cash, UPI and cards. The bill is sent to your WhatsApp through InvoiceSnap and can be paid by scanning the UPI QR code. A 5% GST is added to the bill. We do not charge any service charge; tips are optional.
